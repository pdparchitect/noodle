Noodle

Personal AI agents that get things done. Give a bot a task, or bring several into a group to work toward a shared goal. Noodle runs on the AI subscriptions you already have.

This package installs Noodle and the optional companions you choose. Installing keeps your bots, settings and data, and leaves the app alone if you already have a newer version.

Noodle Computer

Linux, macOS and Windows computers for you and your agents, so a bot can work on a desktop of its own.

Noodle Browser

Dedicated browsers you sign in to and assign to agents, with persistent profiles, tabs, history, bookmarks and file transfers.

Noodle Applet

Run the tools, websites, experiments and games that you and your agents create.

Noodle Terminal

The noodle command: chat with the bots and groups on your Noodle Hub from a terminal. It is installed in /usr/local/bin.
