The apps you chose are in your Applications folder.

If you installed Noodle, open it and follow Settings › Harness to install and sign in to a harness, then create your first bot.

Each app asks for the permissions it needs, such as Accessibility, Screen Recording or Login Items, the first time it needs them.

The apps keep themselves up to date. To update the noodle command, open the latest Noodle Suite Installer again.
