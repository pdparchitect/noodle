Noodle Hub

Runs bots on this Mac for the people you pair with it. It lives in the menu bar. Plans decide which harnesses each person can use, and Usage shows what their bots use.

Noodle Computer

Linux, macOS and Windows computers that the Hub's bots work in. Their owners make them from Noodle, and they run on this Mac.

Noodle Browser

Browsers the Hub's bots use, signed in by their owners, with persistent profiles, tabs, history, bookmarks and file transfers.

Noodle Applet

Runs the tools, websites, experiments and games that the Hub's bots create.

Noodle Terminal

The noodle command: chat with the bots and groups on your Noodle Hub from a terminal. It is installed in /usr/local/bin.

Already have some of these apps? Installing keeps your bots, settings and data, and leaves an app alone if you already have a newer version.
