Noodle Hub and the apps you chose are in your Applications folder.

Open Noodle Hub. It lives in the menu bar, and its welcome ends in pairing your first device.

Keep this Mac on and connected so people can reach their bots: an always-on Mac. Setting up Noodle Hub

Each app asks for the permissions it needs, such as Accessibility, Screen Recording or Login Items, the first time it needs them.

The apps keep themselves up to date. To update the noodle command, open the latest Noodle Hub Installer again.
