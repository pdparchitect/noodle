Noodle Enterprise

Noodle, managed by your organisation.

Deploy Noodle to your people's Macs and decide what it may do, with a configuration profile from the device management you already use.

Read the deployment guide Licence ›

Works with Jamf Pro, Kandji, Microsoft Intune, Mosyle, or a profile installed by hand.

Applies while Noodle runs

Install, change or remove a profile and Noodle follows within a minute, without a restart.

Removed cleanly

What a profile forbids stops, but nothing is deleted. Remove the profile and everyone's own settings come back.

Clear in the app

Settings your profile controls are locked, and Settings › General lists every policy in force under Managed by your organisation.

Seven things you control.

Every setting is optional. Without a profile, Noodle works as it always does.

Harnesses

Choose which harnesses people can use. Agents set to any other don't start.

Unrestricted access

Decide whether agents may run outside the macOS sandbox. When you don't allow it, every agent runs sandboxed.

Tool connections

Turn MCP tool connections off, or allow and block them by catalogue entry and by server address.

Browsers and computers

Decide whether people and their agents can use Noodle Browser and Noodle Computer from Noodle.

Hosting a Hub

Decide whether people can turn on Let My Devices Reach This Mac, which serves their own devices from their Mac.

Connecting to Hubs

Decide whether people can join Noodle Hubs, and limit Noodle to your own Hub's addresses.

Updates

Fix automatic checks and installs on or off, or turn off Noodle's own updates and leave them to your MDM.

Free for personal use and small businesses.

Noodle Enterprise is under the PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0. The rest of Noodle stays free and open source under the Apache 2.0 licence.

Personal use and small businesses

Personal use is free. So are businesses with fewer than 100 people, employees and contractors together, and under US$1 million in revenue the year before (2019 dollars, adjusted for inflation).

Larger organisations ask us

Larger organisations need a licence from us to manage Noodle with profiles. Running Noodle without a profile needs none. For now we grant it free or on favourable terms, so you can deploy Noodle today without worrying about licence costs.

No keys, no checks

Noodle doesn't check for a licence or limit managed settings without one.
