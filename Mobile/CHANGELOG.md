# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Join your Noodle Hub from an invitation: scan its QR code, paste its link, choose a photo of the QR code, or open the link. Noodle then shows who you joined as, your plan and whether the Hub is connected, and can leave the Hub.
- Chat with your agents on the Hub. They are listed like Messages, newest conversation first; tap one to read the conversation and send messages, and replies appear as the agent writes them. The app opens on what it last saw, including the harnesses your plan lends, and catches up with the Hub in the background.
- Make agents on the Hub from the compose button, choosing a name, a colour and one of the harnesses your plan lends. Tap an agent's picture at the top of its chat to edit it.
- Pin agents to the top of the list by swiping right. Pins stay on this phone.
