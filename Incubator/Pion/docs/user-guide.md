# Using Pion

Pion puts a team of AI agents to work on your security: they monitor your
environment, investigate incidents and recommend responses, using the tools you
connect. Agents never change a system themselves: a change waits for your
approval.

## Getting started

1. Open Pion and sign in to a harness on the welcome screen. Pion sets up a
   starter team of agents and the group they share.
2. Connect the services your team should use in **Settings → Tools**, and
   assign each to the agents that need it.
3. Choose **File → New Investigation** (⌘N), say what happened, and pick the
   team. Or just tell an agent what you have seen: when it finds something worth
   a record, it opens the Investigation itself and leads it.
