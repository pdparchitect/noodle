# Using Rook

Rook puts a team of AI agents to work on an authorized offensive engagement:
they map the target, find and demonstrate weaknesses, and write up what they
found. Nothing intrusive happens without your go-ahead.

## Getting started

1. Open Rook and sign in to a harness on the welcome screen. Rook sets up a
   starter team of agents and the group they share.
2. Choose **File → New Mission**, give it a target and a scope, and pick the
   team. Or just tell an agent what to look at: when it finds something worth a
   record, it opens the Mission itself and leads it.
3. Open the **Graph** in the toolbar to see how far a finding reaches.
