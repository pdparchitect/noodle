# Voice messages

Voice recording requires macOS 26 and Apple's on-device transcription support for
your Mac and system language. Noodle may download a speech model on first use.
Transcription runs on your Mac.

1. Click the microphone beside Send and allow microphone access.
2. Speak, then press **Return** to send.
3. To review first, click **Stop**. Press **Return** when ready, or **Escape** to discard.

Choose a microphone in **Settings → General**. If Noodle reports no sound, check
the microphone selection and hardware mute switch. Recordings stop after ten
minutes without sending automatically.

Switching chats stops recording and keeps the draft with its chat, including
after relaunch. If transcription fails, choose **Retry Transcription** or
**Send Audio Only**.

Sent messages show an audio player. Right-click and choose **Show Transcript** to
read or copy the text. Bots receive the audio and any available transcript.
